# Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Frok project maintainers handle data for the hosted Frok service (for example, the shared public edge such as `edge.frok.it`) and related project website(s) (collectively, the "Service").

This policy does not govern self-hosted Frok deployments you run yourself.

## 1. Data We Process

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process:

- connection and routing metadata (timestamps, route hostname labels, protocol type, response/status metadata, and approximate traffic volume);
- network metadata (such as source IP address and destination route used to operate and secure the Service);
- authentication and identity metadata (for example, key fingerprints and stable identity identifiers required for access control and route ownership);
- operational logs (service health, errors, and abuse-prevention events).

Traffic payloads are proxied in transit as needed to provide tunneling, and may be processed transiently for routing, reliability, and security.

## 2. How We Use Data

We use data to:

- provide and maintain the Service;
- authenticate users and enforce route ownership;
- detect, prevent, and investigate abuse, fraud, and security incidents;
- debug and improve reliability/performance;
- comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Legal Bases (Where Applicable)

Where required by law, we process data based on legitimate interests (operating and securing the Service), contract/performance (providing requested service), legal obligations, or your consent where required.

## 4. Retention

We keep data only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above, including security and abuse prevention. Retention periods may vary by data type and operational need.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data:

- with infrastructure/service providers that help operate the Service;
- when required by law, legal process, or valid governmental request;
- to protect rights, safety, and the security of the Service and users.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, but no system is completely secure.

## 7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we apply reasonable safeguards for transfers.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of your personal data. To submit a request, open an issue at:

- `https://github.com/IzioDev/frok/issues`

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy by posting a revised version in this repository. The updated policy is effective on the posted "Effective date."
